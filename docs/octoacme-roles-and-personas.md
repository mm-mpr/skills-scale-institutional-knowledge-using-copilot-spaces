# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
The QA/Testing Lead owns the quality strategy and validates that delivered work meets acceptance criteria. They partner with Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define test approaches and maintain test plans for features and releases
- Validate acceptance criteria and coordinate exploratory, regression, and smoke testing
- Report defects, coverage gaps, and quality risks
- Coordinate with Developers to reproduce defects and verify fixes

### Goals
- Find defects early and reduce production issues
- Provide clear evidence that requirements and quality standards are met
- Improve confidence in releases without delaying useful feedback

### Typical Communication
- Reviews acceptance criteria and testability with Product Managers during refinement
- Collaborates with Developers throughout implementation and defect triage
- Shares test status, release readiness, and quality risks with Project Managers and DevOps/Release Engineers

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead/Architect guides technical direction, architecture, and engineering practices. They help Developers deliver maintainable solutions and advise Product Managers and Project Managers on feasibility, dependencies, and technical risk.

### Responsibilities
- Review designs and guide architectural and integration decisions
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and opportunities to reduce technical debt
- Establish and promote engineering standards and provide technical mentorship
- Advise on estimates and implementation options

### Goals
- Keep the solution secure, scalable, and maintainable
- Reduce rework and unmanaged technical debt
- Enable predictable delivery through sound technical decisions

### Typical Communication
- Works with Developers through design discussions, pairing, and code reviews
- Explains feasibility and trade-offs to Product Managers during prioritization
- Raises technical dependencies and risks with Project Managers during planning and delivery

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
The Stakeholder/Sponsor represents business interests, provides strategic context, and supports governance decisions. They work with Product Managers on value and priorities and rely on Project Managers for delivery status and escalations.

### Responsibilities
- Clarify business outcomes, constraints, and success measures
- Approve scope, funding, or milestone decisions within their authority
- Resolve escalated decisions and help secure resources
- Communicate project outcomes and impacts to broader stakeholder groups

### Goals
- Ensure work remains aligned with strategy and delivers expected value
- Make timely decisions and manage business risk
- Maintain stakeholder confidence through transparent governance

### Typical Communication
- Meets with Product Managers to align outcomes and review priorities
- Receives milestone updates and escalation requests from Project Managers
- Joins reviews and demos with Developers and the delivery team to provide business feedback

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
The Security Lead helps teams identify and manage security and compliance risks across design, implementation, and release. They advise Developers, Product Managers, Project Managers, and DevOps/Release Engineers on security requirements and controls.

### Responsibilities
- Define or clarify security requirements and conduct threat assessments
- Review security-sensitive designs and coordinate security testing and scanning
- Advise on vulnerability remediation, compliance needs, and incident response
- Track security risks and confirm required controls before release

### Goals
- Prevent vulnerabilities and reduce security exposure
- Integrate security into normal delivery practices
- Meet applicable security and compliance requirements

### Typical Communication
- Works with Developers and Technical Leads on secure design and remediation
- Advises Product Managers on security implications of product decisions
- Escalates risks to Project Managers and coordinates scanning and release controls with DevOps/Release Engineers

---

## DevOps/Release Engineer

### Role Summary
The DevOps/Release Engineer maintains delivery infrastructure and supports safe, reliable releases. They work with Developers, QA/Testing Leads, Security Leads, and Project Managers to prepare and operate deployments.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines, environments, and deployment automation
- Coordinate release execution, monitoring, and post-deployment verification
- Maintain rollback, backup, and operational readiness procedures
- Integrate automated quality and security checks into delivery pipelines

### Goals
- Make deployments repeatable and reliable
- Minimize downtime and recover quickly when releases fail
- Improve observability and operational readiness

### Typical Communication
- Coordinates pipeline and environment needs with Developers and Technical Leads
- Plans smoke tests and readiness checks with QA/Testing Leads
- Aligns release timing and operational constraints with Product Managers
- Confirms security controls with Security Leads and shares deployment status with Project Managers

---

## Product Operations Manager

### Role Summary
The Product Operations Manager (optional for larger programs) coordinates practices, insights, and dependencies across multiple product teams. They enable Product Managers and Project Managers to work consistently and make informed program-level decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain shared product processes, tools, and portfolio-level metrics
- Coordinate cross-team dependencies and surface program-level risks
- Improve access to customer, delivery, and operational insights
- Identify process improvements and support consistent adoption across teams

### Goals
- Improve coordination and decision-making across product programs
- Reduce duplicated effort and cross-team friction
- Provide reliable insights into product and program outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Aligns with Product Managers on shared practices, metrics, and customer insights
- Coordinates dependencies and escalations with Project Managers
- Shares relevant priorities and operational context with Developers and delivery teams

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
The Scrum Master/Agile Coach (optional) facilitates team practices and helps remove impediments without taking ownership of product priorities or delivery decisions. They support Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers in improving collaboration and flow.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate planning, daily coordination, reviews, and retrospectives as appropriate
- Help the team identify and resolve impediments and workflow bottlenecks
- Coach team members on effective agile practices and continuous improvement
- Encourage transparent work tracking and sustainable team practices

### Goals
- Enable a collaborative, self-organizing team
- Improve delivery flow and make blockers visible early
- Turn retrospectives into measurable, ongoing improvements

### Typical Communication
- Facilitates regular team events and helps Developers coordinate day-to-day work
- Partners with Product Managers on refinement and clear, prioritized work
- Coordinates impediment escalation and delivery-process improvements with Project Managers
